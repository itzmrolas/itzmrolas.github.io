Introduction

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for itzmrolas.eu (published at https://itzmrolas.eu/privacypolicy).

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data may be collected, processed, and stored when you visit or interact with this personal website, and provides details regarding your data protection rights under applicable regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2

Website Operator & Controller

The data controller responsible for processing activities on itzmrolas.eu is Aleksander Świgost (operating online under the moniker itzmrolas), an individual residing in Poland.

This website is maintained as a personal website. For any privacy requests, inquiries, or data protection questions, you can contact the controller directly via email at:

aleksanderswigost@gmail.com
3

Personal Data Processed

When you voluntarily choose to sign in to itzmrolas.eu using Google Sign-In, the website processes the following user account information provided through Google:

Display Name
The profile name associated with your Google account.
Email Address
Your email address supplied during authentication.
Profile Picture URL
A web link pointing to your account avatar to render your profile picture.
Firebase User ID (UID)
A unique identifier assigned to your Firebase user account.
4

Google Sign-In & Firebase Authentication

User login functionality on this website is facilitated through Firebase Authentication (a service provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC).

Authentication occurs directly through Google's secure authentication flow. This website never receives, handles, or stores your Google account password.

Google/Firebase processes authentication data as part of providing cloud authentication infrastructure. Where data is transferred internationally outside the European Economic Area (EEA), such transfers rely on standard contractual safeguards established by Google, such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

5

Purpose of Processing

Personal data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate users and manage active website user sessions.
  • To display user profile details (such as account display name and avatar) in the user interface.
  • To maintain website reliability, security, and address direct inquiries sent to the controller.
6

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

Processing activities conducted on this website are grounded in specific legal bases under Article 6(1) of the GDPR:

Performance of a Contract / Steps at User Request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) Used when you choose to log in via Google Sign-In, enabling the website to authenticate your identity and provide account functionality requested by you.
Legitimate Interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) Used for basic security monitoring, ensuring website operational integrity, preventing unauthorized access, and responding to voluntary communications sent to the website operator.
7

Data Retention & Erasure

Firebase authentication records are stored as long as your account remains active in the authentication database.

You may submit a request to delete your account data at any time by contacting aleksanderswigost@gmail.com.

Please note that the right to erasure under Article 17 GDPR is not absolute. Certain data may be retained where required by applicable legal obligations or legitimate exceptions (such as resolving active security issues or legal claims).

8

Third-Party Recipients

Personal data processed in connection with this site may be shared with service providers necessary to operate website functions:

  • Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC: Provides Firebase Authentication and related Google services used by the website.
  • Payment Providers (PayPal / Banks): Interacted with directly and voluntarily by users who choose to send donations.

Personal data is not sold, rented, or disclosed to unauthorized third-party commercial brokers.

9

Google Fonts

This webpage loads typography styles dynamically using Google Fonts. When your web browser loads the font files remotely, it connects directly to Google's content servers.

This connection transmits technical request headers (including your IP address and browser User-Agent details) to Google to deliver font files to your browser.

Browser Storage & Technical Data

Data Security

This website uses Transport Layer Security (HTTPS encryption) to protect network communication between your web browser and site servers.

While reasonable technical measures are employed, absolute security cannot be guaranteed for data transmissions over the open Internet.

Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR legislation, you hold specific data protection rights subject to statutory conditions:

Right of Access (Art. 15) Request confirmation and copies of personal data processed about you.
Right to Rectification (Art. 16) Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
Right to Erasure (Art. 17) Request deletion of your personal data where statutory conditions apply.
Right to Restriction (Art. 18) Request restriction of data processing in specific legal circumstances.
Right to Data Portability (Art. 20) Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used machine-readable format.
Right to Object (Art. 21) Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
Supervisory Authority Complaint Right You have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority. In Poland, the competent supervisory authority is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
